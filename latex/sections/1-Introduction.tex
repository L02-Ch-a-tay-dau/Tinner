\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\subsection{Problem Statement}
In modern daily life, particularly for students, office workers, and couples, the question ``What should we eat today?'' has evolved from a simple inquiry into a daily source of stress. This issue manifests in three major pain points:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Decision Fatigue:} On average, individuals spend 15 to 20 minutes aimlessly scrolling through food delivery applications without making a final choice, often resulting in choosing a random, unsatisfactory meal or skipping it entirely.
    \item \textbf{Choice Overload (The Paradox of Choice):} Existing platforms display hundreds of restaurant options simultaneously in a list format. This overwhelming amount of information causes cognitive overload, making it harder for users to decide and leaving them less satisfied with their eventual choice.
    \item \textbf{Social Friction:} Deciding on a dining location is a common source of conflict for groups, frequently leading to frustration or uneven compromises.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Objectives and Purpose}
The primary objective of this project is to develop \textbf{Tinner} (a portmanteau of ``Tinder'' and ``Dinner''), a mobile application designed to solve the ``Analysis Paralysis'' problem in food selection. Guided by the slogan \textit{``Swipe To Bite''}, Tinner simplifies the decision-making process. 

The core purposes of the application include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Streamlining Decisions:} Helping users transition from opening the application to deciding on a meal in under 5 minutes.
    \item \textbf{Visual-First Experience:} Providing a ``Single-View Interface'' that displays one high-quality dish image at a time, removing distractions and stimulating the appetite.
    \item \textbf{Gamified Discovery:} Implementing a swipe-based mechanism (Swipe Left to pass, Swipe Right to save/like) that transforms a tedious chore into an engaging activity.
    \item \textbf{Seamless Information Access:} Upon liking a dish, instantly presenting a Restaurant Info Card with crucial details (address, opening hours, map integration, and reviews) to finalize the decision.
    \item \textbf{Actionable Management:} Allowing users to maintain a personal ``Want to Try'' list to review, compare, and navigate to selected restaurants efficiently.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Real-world Need and Potential Impact}
Comprehensive market research indicates a significant gap in the current ecosystem of food-related applications. While delivery super-apps excel in logistics, social media platforms excel in visual inspiration, and mapping services excel in location data, there is no dominant hybrid model optimized purely for fast, enjoyable, and structured restaurant discovery.

Tinner addresses this real-world need by positioning itself as a ``Decision Engine'' rather than a transactional middleman. By focusing on the discovery phase and incorporating features like Smart Filters and Personal Collections, the application reduces cognitive load and bridges the gap between structured information and engaging user experience.

The potential impact of Tinner is substantial, especially among Gen Z and Millennials. It promises to drastically reduce the time wasted on daily dining decisions, alleviate the social friction associated with group choices, and provide an intuitive and fun solution to an everyday dilemma.
